How to Take Accurate Measurements

Keep measuring tape snug: Do not pull too tight. You should be able to slide two fingers underneath.

Wear underlying undergarments: Always wear the exact style of bra/shapewear intended under the final outfit.

Tie a ribbon around the waist: This highlights the natural narrow waistline for reliable length measurements.
